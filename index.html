Meet Ayebatonye

Ayebatonye Emmanuel, popularly known as Ayebatonye, is from Brass Local Government Area of Bayelsa State, Nigeria. He studied Geology at the Federal University Otuoke and has interests spanning fashion designing and construction.

His profile brings together an academic interest in the earth sciences, a creative approach to design, and an interest in the practical world of construction. He also has a public presence and community connection in Twon-Brass.
